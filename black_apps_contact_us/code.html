Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, upfront answers on partner delegated access, confidentiality, and commercial terms.

01

How does Granular Admin Privileges (GDAP) delegation work?

We operate strictly within Microsoft's Zero Trust security guidelines. We request granular, time-bound GDAP relationships tailored exclusively to Business Central environments without requiring Global Admin credentials in your primary Azure Tenant. Access can be revoked anytime via your Microsoft 365 Admin Center.

02

Can we switch Business Central partners without business disruption?

Yes. Partner of Record (CSP) transitions do not involve codebase redeployments or service interruptions. We execute a parallel audit phase, clone your sandbox environments, verify all custom AL extensions and telemetry pipelines, and transition production oversight seamlessly over a designated weekend window.

03

When is the NDA executed and who owns custom AL code?

We sign standard mutual NDAs prior to accessing your architecture or production telemetry logs. Furthermore, all custom AL code, page extensions, API integrations, and Power BI semantic models developed by Black Apps are 100% owned by your organization with complete source control in your private Git repositories.

04

What are your consulting engagement models and response SLAs?

We provide structured, milestone-based fixed fees for Project Rescue and Green-field Implementations. For ongoing technical support and AL engineering, we provide SLA-backed monthly retainers featuring guaranteed 1-hour critical response windows, dedicated named architects, and unutilized hour rollover.
