Support Operational Protocols

Clear procedural expectations so your internal finance, operations, and IT leads have complete transparency.

tune

How do rollover hours work?

Unused support engineering hours do not vanish at month’s end. Depending on your tier, unused time rolls into the subsequent 30-60 day cycles and can be allocated toward pipeline improvements, custom RDLC reporting, or Power Automate flows.

security

How do we grant tenant access safely?

We utilize Microsoft Granular Delegated Admin Privileges (GDAP) with Azure PIM (Privileged Identity Management) just-in-time elevation. We never use static shared global administrator credentials or unsecured accounts.

schema

What happens during Microsoft Wave Updates?

We orchestrate early release validation in an isolated Microsoft BC preview environment. We review all breaking AL changes, recompile your bespoke extensions, and verify third-party AppSource compatibility before production scheduling.

groups

Can our team speak with the same consultants?

Yes. Every client is assigned a designated Primary Solution Architect and a Secondary Systems Engineer. You work with engineers who understand your chart of accounts, dimension structure, and warehouse routing logic.
